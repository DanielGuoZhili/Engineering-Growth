# Instructions for coding agents

- Work on **only the current weekly scope** unless asked otherwise. Avoid creating a full multi-month plan.
- Never fake evidence of independent skill. Mark contributions as SOLO, ASSISTED, or AGENT-BUILT.
- For assessment tasks, provide instructions and tests without exposing solutions until the user completes an honest attempt.
- Prefer minimal dependencies and runnable, tested examples.
- For each change: briefly explain its purpose, how to run/verify, and what the learner must explain independently.
- Do not edit other personal repositories without explicit permission.
- Never copy confidential employer code, workplace logs, credentials, or proprietary artifacts.
- Do not auto-commit solutions to an assessment before the learner has tried it.
- Weekly reports must distinguish observed facts from estimates and require evidence links.
