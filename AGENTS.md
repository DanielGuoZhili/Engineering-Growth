# Instructions for coding agents

- Work on **only the current weekly scope** unless asked otherwise. Avoid creating a full multi-month plan.
- Never fake evidence of independent skill. Mark contributions as SOLO, ASSISTED, or AGENT-BUILT.
- For assessment tasks, provide instructions and tests without exposing solutions until the user completes an honest attempt.
- Prefer minimal dependencies and runnable, tested examples.
- For each change: briefly explain its purpose, how to run/verify, and what the learner must explain independently.
- Do not edit other personal repositories without explicit permission.
- Never copy confidential employer code, workplace logs, credentials, or proprietary artifacts.
- Do not auto-commit solutions to an assessment before the learner has tried it.
- Weekly reports must distinguish observed facts from estimates and require evidence links.

## Personal notes

- When working anywhere under `notes/`, read and follow the more specific role and workflow in `notes/AGENTS.md`.
- Treat `notes/` as the learner's personal knowledge base. Preserve the meaning and first-person voice of raw notes; improve structure and wording without inventing experience, conclusions, or certainty.
- Put fragments with no clear home in `notes/inbox.md`. Add a date and a few retrieval tags; do not force premature categorization.
- Keep algorithm code, first-attempt evidence, and exercise-specific review in `practice/algorithms/<problem>/`. When a temp note is formally organized, put the polished learning record and Agent response in the current `notes/主笔记-N.md`, then link to the practice evidence instead of copying it without attribution.
- Keep the learner's original idea separate from later AI feedback. Never rewrite an assisted solution as an independent insight.
- When organizing notes, prefer small links and indexes over moving or rewriting large amounts of user-authored text without need.
