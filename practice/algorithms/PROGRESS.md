## Status vocabulary

- **Not started**: No independent attempt yet.
- **Attempted**: First attempt saved, regardless of correctness.
- **Tested**: Actual test/LeetCode results recorded.
- **Reviewed**: Both implementations compared and findings documented.
- **Revisit**: Needs a no-hints retry on a later date.

## Monthly / weekly review signals

- Sessions actually completed (target: >=1/week, not a quota of accepted solutions)
- Elapsed time, including English prompt-reading time
- JS and Python **solo vs. assisted** status
- Big O explanation and edge-case coverage
- Specific Python-vs-JS syntax / data-structure gaps
- One concrete improvement to verify in the next session

Never mark a problem passed solely because the AI's code passes.
